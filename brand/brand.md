# blocmind brand

**blocmind** (always lowercase) is a product studio. Tagline: **Small tools for everyday life.** Site: blocmind.com · support@blocmind.com.

Name: a *bloc* is one solid, well-made piece; a *mind* is the care in it. The mark is a 2×2 grid: three ink rounded squares and one green circle (top right).
