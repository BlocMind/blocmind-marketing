# blocmind · 7-day posting schedule (Wed 18 – Tue 24 Nov 2026)

All times Asia/Colombo. Each slot folder holds `facebook.png` (Facebook) and `tiktok-1.jpg …` (TikTok carousel, post in order).
Same post goes to Facebook and TikTok at the same time.

## Posting times

| Slot | Time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Story | **10:00** | Highest-scoring hour for this brand on both TikTok and Facebook, every day of the week (Metricool best-time data) |
| Midday | **12:00** | Second peak on both networks (lunch scroll) |
| Evening | **18:00** | TikTok's third peak; Facebook still strong (after work) |

Weekends score about 40% lower than weekdays at the same hours, but the peaks stay at 10:00, 12:00 and 18:00. On Sunday, Facebook's evening (18:00) is as strong as midday.
The daily task currently posts at 19:30. With this schedule it would add a 4th post, so pause it for the week or move it to 18:00 and let it post the 18:00 slot.
